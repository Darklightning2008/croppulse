Parameters used in the prototype

The app compares temperature, humidity, rainfall, leaf wetness, minimum temperature, wind and soil moisture against disease-favouring conditions, plus a bonus when the crop is at a sensitive growth stage. Variety susceptibility and infected planting material should also be included in a validated model.
